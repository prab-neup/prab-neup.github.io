What it does

Discern is a browser extension for researchers who read across many sources. Capture highlights from any web page or PDF as you go, then run the reconciliation pipeline: it extracts the claims in your sources, compares them against each other, and shows you exactly where they agree, disagree, or say nothing at all — always anchored to a verified quote from the original text, never an unexplained conclusion.

Capture

Highlight text on any page or PDF. Tags, comments, and citation metadata are captured automatically. Free, forever.

Reconcile

Extracts claims across your captured sources and clusters them into agreement, disagreement, and gaps in coverage.

Verify

Every AI-surfaced claim is checked against your original captured text before it's ever shown to you.

Bring your own key

Uses your own Google Gemini API key. Calls go straight from your device to Google — we never see your data or your usage.
