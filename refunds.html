Refund Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Discern gives every account a free 7-day trial with full access to the reconciliation pipeline — every feature you'd get after paying, unlocked, before you spend anything. Because of that, we don't offer refunds once a purchase is completed.

Why

The trial exists specifically so you can fully evaluate whether the tool is useful for your own research before paying for it. A purchase made after that evaluation window is final.

The one exception

If a genuine technical problem on our end prevents you from using the product after purchase — something actually broken, not a feature working differently than you expected — contact us and we'll make it right, which may include a refund at our discretion.

How purchases are processed

Payments are handled by Paddle.com, acting as merchant of record. Any refund we approve is issued through Paddle using your original payment method.
